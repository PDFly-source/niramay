# Niramay Reuse Policy

This policy explains who may do what with Niramay's original source code and
assets. It complements [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Allowed to the project owner

The project owner may reuse their original project code and assets freely,
including in:

- future Niramay versions;
- other personal projects;
- commercial projects and products;
- private, closed-source projects;
- other websites and applications;
- derivative works of any kind;
- private or public forks of their own work;
- future releases under different license terms (relicensing).

## Not automatically allowed to third parties

Because Niramay is **not** an open-source project, third parties may not:

- copy the repository;
- publish a modified copy of it;
- create a competing or rebranded version from the source;
- redistribute, sell, or sublicense the source;
- claim Niramay's source as their own;

unless the project owner grants explicit permission. Public visibility of the
repository does not imply any of these permissions.

## Third-party components

This policy concerns the project owner's original material only.
Third-party components and assets remain governed by their respective
licenses and are not covered by the project's proprietary ownership claim.
(React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, the bundled fonts, and the other packages
listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md are examples of such third-party material.)

Copyright also does not extend to the underlying traditional home-remedy
knowledge itself: it belongs to its communities and traditions. Niramay's
proprietary protection covers its original code, writing, organization, UI
implementation, and project-owned artwork only.

## A technical reality check

Niramay is a client-side web application: code delivered to a browser can be
inspected with developer tools, and no license can technically prevent
someone from reading or copying browser-delivered JavaScript. The law and
this license govern **permission**, not technical access. "The repository is
publicly viewable" and "the source is not licensed for reuse" are both true
at the same time.

## Using the deployed app

Everyone is welcome to use the deployed Niramay web application
([live app](https://pdfly-source.github.io/niramay/)) for its intended
personal and educational purposes, including installing it as a PWA. That
usage is separate from source-code rights and remains permitted.

## Permission requests

For reuse or licensing permission, contact the project owner through the
[repository](https://github.com/PDFly-source/niramay).
